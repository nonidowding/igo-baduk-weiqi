# How to Play Go
The objective of capture go was to capture stones. In a full game of go the objective is to control territory. A point of territory is an empty intersection of the board surrounded by a single color of stones. Play revolves around negotiating for space on the board. Capture is still a part of the game and can aid in securing territory, but it is not the primary focus.

The rules are similar to capture go but not exactly the same: 
1. At the start of the game the board is empty.
2. Black plays first. They may play anywhere to start.
3. White and black then alternate turns placing a single stone on the board. For the most part they may play anywhere but there are the cases of the no "self-capture" rule, and the "ko" rule.
4. Play continues until both players pass their turn in succession.
5. The game is counted and scored. Whoever has more points wins. 

A common question that arises when new players are trying out their first games is, when is the game over? When is the right time to pass? The easy answer is, when there are no more beneficial moves left on the board. Knowing how to determine that is a little harder.
